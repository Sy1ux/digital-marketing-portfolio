The Method

A repeatable performance-marketing loop used across TikTok Shop and Shopify campaigns — from spotting the right product to scaling what works.

  1. 1. Research

    Find the right product for the season — trend/demand signals, competitor and creative research (AdSpy), and seasonal timing.

  2. 2. Create

    Produce the content — creatives, product pages, and campaign assets using the content generation tool stack.

  3. 3. Budget

    Set the ad campaign budget — spend allocation, targeting, and campaign structure across ad platforms.

  4. 4. Launch

    Go live and monitor — track early performance signals (spend, conversions, revenue) as the campaign runs.

  5. 5. Optimize

    Iterate or scale based on results — kill underperformers, double down on winners, and feed learnings back into Research.
